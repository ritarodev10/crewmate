## Generation Notes

**For all job photos:**
- Keep the same camera position for before and after pairs so they feel clearly matched
- "Before" photos: available light only, slightly imperfect framing, no staging
- "After" photos: same angle, slightly warmer feel, clean and tidy, work is complete

**For all avatars:**
- Consistent lighting style across all 10 — either all natural light or all studio
- Consistent crop: face fills ~60% of the square, chin to top of head
- No identical backgrounds — vary between outdoor blurred, indoor window, neutral grey
- Ethnically Italian (Mediterranean) appearance for all; varied ages 28–46

**Seed usage:**
Photos are referenced in `JobType.customerPhotoUrls` and `JobType.workerPhotoUrls` in the seed script.
After generating, place in `apps/web/public/images/` and update the seed URLs to use `/images/jobs/{filename}`.
